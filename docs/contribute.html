Contribute to Our Project

We welcome fellow Pokémon enthusiasts and data science experts to contribute to our evolving project! Whether you're interested in analyzing Pokémon data, enhancing user experience, or dreaming of building something extraordinary, there's a place for you here.

Our Current Goals

  • Extend Analysis: Analyze and incorporate more types of Pokémon into our database to broaden our findings.
  • Improve Web Design: Help us make our website more interactive and visually appealing for all users.
  • Create a Pokémon Movement Tracker: Develop a system that tracks how Pokémon move in the real world, based on environmental factors.
  • Long-Term Goal: Create a realistic version of Pokémon GO, allowing users to complete the Pokédex in real life. Pokémon will be located in regions that align with their environmental traits, often in remote or extreme locations.

If you're interested in learning more about our progress or how you can help, feel free to contact Vignesh Narayanan at v.narayanan@lse.ac.uk.
