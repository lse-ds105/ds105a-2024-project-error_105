Contribute to Our Project

Join our team of Pokémon enthusiasts and data scientists! Whether you're an expert in Pokémon data analysis, web development, or have a creative flair, we have a place for you. Together, we can bring this ambitious project to life!

Our Current Goals

  • Extend Analysis: Expand our database with more Pokémon types to improve our research findings.
  • Enhance Web Design: Help us make our platform more user-friendly and visually engaging.
  • Pokémon Movement Tracker: Build a system that tracks real-world Pokémon movements based on environmental conditions.
  • Live Weather Updates: We're working on integrating live weather updates to make the map refreshable based on real-time conditions. If you're interested in contributing to this feature, we'd love to collaborate!
  • Long-Term Vision: Create a realistic Pokémon GO-like experience where users can complete the Pokédex in real life by discovering Pokémon in their natural habitats.

Exciting Update: We are actively working on adding live weather data to enhance the map's interactivity. Stay tuned if you're interested in helping us build this feature!

If you're interested in learning more or would like to get involved, please reach out to Vignesh Narayanan at v.narayanan@lse.ac.uk.
