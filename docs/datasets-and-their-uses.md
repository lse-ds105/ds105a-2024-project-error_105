### Datasets and Their Uses

#### Google Earth Engine Dataset:
This dataset provides a comprehensive classification of world ecoregions and biomes, which will enable us to map Pokémon types to corresponding habitats (e.g., deserts for Fire types, tundra for Ice types). Its global coverage and ecological accuracy are key for realistic Pokémon distribution.

#### Open-Meteo API:
This API supplies real-time environmental data such as temperature, precipitation, and other climate factors. We will use this data to scale Pokémon placement within biomes, ranking Pokémon based on their power and adaptability to extreme conditions (e.g., Charizard in hotter desert areas compared to Charmander).

#### Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF):
The GBIF dataset offers insight into real-world species distribution, allowing us to compare Pokémon characteristics with similar species. For example, Magikarp can be linked to habitats of real carp species, enhancing the ecological realism of our project.

#### Pokémon API (PokeAPI):
This API provides detailed information on Pokémon attributes such as types, stats, and abilities. We will use it to extract relevant data for biome matching and power scaling, ensuring the project is grounded in Pokémon lore and mechanics (e.g., identifying Fire-type Pokémon and ranking them by their stats).
