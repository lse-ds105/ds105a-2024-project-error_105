Introduction

This project analyzes how Pokémon types—Fire, Water, and Ice—might be distributed in the real world based on their natural affinities for specific climates and biomes.

By leveraging data from Pokémon attributes and real-world environmental metrics, we rank Pokémon and assign them to suitable locations in the real world.

Purpose of the Analysis

The goal is to combine environmental data with Pokémon characteristics to create an engaging and scientifically plausible mapping of Pokémon habitats.

Data Sources Used

  • PokeAPI: Provides Pokémon details such as types, stats, and attributes.
  • Google Earth Engine: Offers detailed biome data, including centroids of over 1,500 biomes.
  • Open-Meteo: Supplies climate data like temperatures and rainfall patterns.
