Welcome to the PokéMap Project!

Mapping Pokémon habitats based on real-world climate and biomes

Introduction

This project combines Pokémon lore with real-world climatological and ecological data to create a scientifically plausible mapping of Pokémon habitats. The primary objective is to determine where Fire, Water, and Ice-type Pokémon would reside using a ranking scale based on their stats and corresponding environmental factors, such as temperature and rainfall. The analysis simulates a "real-world Pokémon map," reflecting extreme environments for the most powerful Pokémon.

  • Fire-Type Pokémon: Mapped to the hottest places on Earth.
  • Water-Type Pokémon: Linked to the wettest regions.
  • Ice-Type Pokémon: Thrive in the coldest environments.

Goal: To integrate Pokémon attributes, climate data, and biomes into a single cohesive map to visualize where Pokémon would live if they existed in the real world.
