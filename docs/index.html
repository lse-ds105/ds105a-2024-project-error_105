Welcome to the PokéMap Project!

Mapping Pokémon habitats based on real-world climate and biomes

Introduction

This project combines Pokémon lore with real-world climatological and ecological data to create a scientifically plausible mapping of Pokémon habitats.

  • Fire-Type Pokémon: Mapped to the hottest places on Earth.
  • Water-Type Pokémon: Linked to the wettest regions.
  • Ice-Type Pokémon: Thrive in the coldest environments.

Goal: To integrate Pokémon attributes, climate data, and biomes into a single cohesive map.

User Guide

This map allows users to explore Pokémon habitats interactively. Click on each feature below to learn more:

▶ Filter Pokémon by Type
You can filter Pokémon habitats by Fire, Water, or Ice types using the toggle button in the top-right corner.
▶ Toggle Heatmap Layer
The heatmap layer can be toggled on/off for better clarity.
▶ Click on Pokémon Icons
Click on Pokémon icons to view their stats, ranking and details.
▶ Use Zoom Controls
Use the zoom controls at the top left to explore specific areas in greater detail.
▶ Toggle Markers
When the map first loads, there are blue markers. You can toggle them to make them disappear from the map for better view.
▶ Search Pokémon
For best results, zoom into the map first, then search for the Pokémon character you want to find. The map will move to the Pokémon and highlight its location with a red circle.
▶ View Pokémon and Weather Info
To see both the weather and Pokémon information, first click on the Pokémon icon, then click on the round weather marker. The Pokémon info will stay on the screen until you click the Pokémon icon again to disable the info popup.
