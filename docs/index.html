Welcome to the PokéMap Project!

Mapping Pokémon habitats based on real-world climate and biomes

Introduction

This project combines Pokémon lore with real-world climatological and ecological data to create a scientifically plausible mapping of Pokémon habitats.

  • Fire-Type Pokémon: Mapped to the hottest places on Earth.
  • Water-Type Pokémon: Linked to the wettest regions.
  • Ice-Type Pokémon: Thrive in the coldest environments.

Goal: To integrate Pokémon attributes, climate data, and biomes into a single cohesive map.

Interactive Map

User Guide

The interactive map offers the following features to enhance your experience:

  • Use the filter menu in the top-right corner to show only Fire, Water, or Ice Pokémon. You can also toggle the heatmap layer on or off.
  • Click on a Pokémon's image on the map to view its ranking and detailed habitat information.
  • Zoom in and out using the map's controls to explore specific regions more closely or get a global view.

Interested in contributing? We are looking to expand our analysis into more Pokémon types! For more details, visit the Contribute page.
